Turn Novels Into Comics

Paste a Chinese novel and Inkstone automatically extracts characters, plans panels, and generates a webtoon or paged comic.

Tap to view full webtoon ↓

L1 — Prompt Hard-Description

Character looks, outfits, and settings are baked into every panel prompt — identity doesn't rely on reference images.

L2 — Reference img2img

Character portraits plus the previous panel feed Agnes as multi-image references, helping keep cross-panel consistency.

Resumable + Fault-Tolerant

Persisted state.json, patient 503 retries, and safe content skipping — long novels finish reliably.

Real generated output, no manual retouching
